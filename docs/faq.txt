GREENLEAF WELLNESS FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ABOUT GREENLEAF WELLNESS
GreenLeaf Wellness is a wellness and spa center with three locations in Italy.
We offer massages, facial treatments, sauna sessions, detox programs and personalized relaxation paths.
Our mission is to help people find balance between body and mind.

OPENING HOURS
Milan location. Monday to Friday from 9 to 20. Saturday from 10 to 18. Sunday closed.
Rome location. Monday to Saturday from 9 to 21. Sunday from 10 to 17.
Naples location. Monday to Friday from 9 to 19. Saturday from 10 to 16. Sunday closed.

HOW TO BOOK AN APPOINTMENT
You can book an appointment in three ways.
First, call the number of your preferred location.
Second, use our mobile app GreenLeaf Wellness available for iOS and Android.
Third, book online on our website www.greenleafwellness.it in the booking section.
We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance for weekend appointments.

CANCELLATION POLICY
You can cancel or reschedule an appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled time at no cost.
Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice are charged 50 percent of the treatment price.
No shows are charged the full price of the treatment.
To cancel, call the location or use the app.

GIFT CARDS
Gift cards are available for any amount starting from 50 euro.
You can buy them online on our website or at any location.
Gift cards are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.
Gift cards can be used for any treatment or product in our centers.
Gift cards are not refundable but can be transferred to another person.

WHAT TO BRING
